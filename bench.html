How the boards are made

Fixed & shared. All models play the same first 3 boards of boards.json, so scores are comparable and reproducible.

Difficult. For each board, 300 random legal fleets were generated and each was played by the probability-density bot (3 times). The fleet that bot needed the most shots to find was kept. Fleets hide where density heuristics look least.

Realistic. Only plausible fleets qualify: standard fleet (5-4-3-3-2), ships straight and non-overlapping, may touch, 2-3 horizontal ships (mixed orientation), at most 2 ships flush against the border.

Score. 100 × (100 − avg shots) / 83. 100 = 17 shots; 0 = not finished in 100.
